---
id: ADR-007
kind: decision
title: Banker's rounding for money amounts
date: 2026-04-11
---
Decision: keep line amounts exact, add them, and round the total once, with halves to
even. This is what `src/invoice/round.rs` must do from now on.

Why: BUG-12 showed that rounding every line lets the errors pile up on long invoices.

Rejected: P-3, rounding halves away from zero. It matches what spreadsheets do, but it
still rounds every line, so it keeps the drift. Our totals will sometimes differ from a
customer's spreadsheet by a cent, and that is the correct answer.

Not related: BUG-3, which is about the export format, not amounts.
